📊 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard | Power BI
📌 Project Overview
The E-Commerce Sales Dashboard is an interactive Business Intelligence solution developed using Power BI. It provides comprehensive insights into sales performance, profitability, customer purchasing behavior, payment preferences, and regional performance.
The dashboard transforms raw sales data into actionable business insights, helping stakeholders monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and make data-driven decisions.
________________________________________
🎯 Project Objectives
•	Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
•	Identify top-performing customers and states.
•	Monitor monthly profit trends.
•	Understand customer payment preferences.
•	Evaluate category and sub-category profitability.
•	Support business decision-making through data visualization.

🔍 Key Insights
1. Payment Method Analysis
•	Cash on Delivery (COD) is the most preferred payment method.
•	UPI is the second most used payment option.
•	Credit Card, Debit Card, and EMI contribute a smaller share of transactions.
2. Customer Analysis
•	Harivansh generated the highest sales amount.
•	Madhav and Madan Mohan are among the top revenue-generating customers.
3. Regional Analysis
•	Maharashtra contributes the highest profit.
•	Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh are other strong-performing states.
4. Category Analysis
•	Clothing contributes the largest share of profit.
•	Electronics and Furniture also play significant roles in overall profitability.
5. Monthly Performance
•	Strong profits observed during the beginning and end of the year.
•	Some months recorded negative profits, indicating potential operational challenges.
6. Sub-Category Analysis
•	Printers generated the highest profit.
•	Bookcases and Sarees also performed well.
